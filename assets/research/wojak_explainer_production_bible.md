**Channel Reference:** [@Wojak-Explainer](https://www.youtube.com/@Wojak-Explainer)  
**Document Purpose:** Comprehensive reference guide and production blueprint for generating high-retention Wojak archetype parody videos, character variations, societal stereotypes, gender/generational dynamics, and audio scoring.

## 1. Core Channel Philosophy & Format Blueprint

The `@Wojak-Explainer` channel specializes in **social archetype anthologies** and **subculture breakdowns**. While channel formats like SapX focus horizontally across nations (*"Every Country's Hospital Be Like"*), Wojak Explainer explores vertically through **human personalities, social habits, life phases, and digital archetypes**.

### Core Video Formats & Title Templates
1. **The "Types of..." Anthology:**
   - *Types of Introverts be like*
   - *Types of Gym Members be like*
   - *Types of Modern Girlfriends / Boyfriends be like*
   - *Types of Coders / Developers be like*
   - *Types of Students during Exams be like*
   - *Types of Drivers on the Road be like*
   - *Types of Music Listeners be like (Parts 1–3)*

2. **The "Every... In a Nutshell" Chronology:**
   - *Every Generation be like (Boomer to Gen Alpha)*
   - *Every Decade of Life be like (Ages 10 to 80)*
   - *Every College Major in a nutshell*
   - *Every Music Artist in a nutshell*

3. **The "POV: Social Friction" Narrative:**
   - *POV: You enter a modern gym in 2026*
   - *POV: Group chats with your friends*
   - *POV: Trying to buy a house in your 20s*

### Cognitive Retention Mechanics
- **Silent Reading Comedy (Zero Voiceover):** Viewers must actively fixate on the screen to read the 12–18 word punchline within 4.25 seconds. Retention regularly reaches 110–135% because viewers rewind to re-read punchlines or examine background visual easter eggs.
- **Micro-Pacing (4.0s to 4.5s per Card):** Each archetype gets a dedicated 4.25-second window. The tempo is brisk; viewers never experience dead air.
- **Stereotype Calibration (Relatable Friction vs. Cruelty):** The humor targets **universal relatable habits** (e.g. introverts canceling plans at the last minute and feeling overwhelming relief; gym bros spending 20 minutes setting up camera tripods; Gen Z speaking purely in TikTok acronyms).

### Detailed Character Profiles

#### 1. The Doomer (The Weary Cynic)
- **Visuals:** Dark charcoal grey hoodie, messy or bald head covered by a knit black beanie, heavy dark hollow circles under eyes with cross-hatched shadowing, 1000-yard stare, cigarette held in hand with curling smoke.
- **Lore & Mentality:** Late 20s/early 30s. Crushed by economic realities, rent prices, and 9-to-5 corporate drudgery. Wanders city streets at 2 AM listening to Russian post-punk.
- **Signature Line:** *"Alarm set for 6:30 AM. Soul left the building in 2019."*

#### 2. The Bloomer (The Mindful Optimist)
- **Visuals:** Fresh-faced classic Wojak, warm upturned smiling mouth, bright eyes, light blue or pastel sweater, running shoes or reusable coffee tumbler.
- **Lore & Mentality:** A former Doomer who stopped doomscrolling, started running at 6 AM, drinks 3 liters of water, reads Marcus Aurelius, and genuinely loves life.
- **Signature Line:** *"Started waking up at 5:30 AM. Life isn't perfect, but the morning coffee is fantastic."*

#### 3. The GigaChad / Nordic Chad (Peak Self-Assurance)
- **Visuals:** Chiseled razor-sharp jawline, golden Nordic beard and sideburns, towering muscular traps, proud posture, white muscle tee or crisp black suit.
- **Lore & Mentality:** Absolute unbothered self-confidence. Rejects social pressure with an unflinching *"Yes."* Treats everyone with quiet, effortless respect.
- **Signature Line:** *"You spent 4 hours arguing on Twitter? I spent 4 hours lifting heavy stones with my brother."*

#### 4. The Tradwife / Trad Girl (The Counter-Culture Traditionalist)
- **Visuals:** Golden blonde braided hair, blue floral prairie dress with white collar, rosy cheeks, wholesome maternal smile, holding a freshly baked loaf of sourdough or apron.
- **Lore & Mentality:** Rejects corporate hustle culture, LinkedIn grindsets, and fast food. Believes in slow living, homesteading, homemaking, and raising family.
- **Signature Line:** *"Your CEO made record profits. Meanwhile, I just baked garlic herb focaccia from scratch."*

#### 5. The Doomer Girl (The Alt Nihilist)
- **Visuals:** Black choker, heavy dark winged eyeliner, disheveled black bob hair with bangs, oversized black knit jumper, black nail polish.
- **Lore & Mentality:** Female counterpart to the Doomer. Listens to Deftones and Phoebe Bridgers, drinks iced black coffee in winter, tired of dating apps.
- **Signature Line:** *"Left on read by 4 different guys who play bass. Going to bed with my heating pad."*

#### 6. The Soyjak / Consoomer (The Hyper-Trend Follower)
- **Visuals:** Wide open screaming oval mouth, thin round wireframe glasses, patchy neckbeard stubble, sparse thinning hair, holding a Nintendo Switch or energy drink.
- **Lore & Mentality:** Consumes product, gets excited for next product. Believes multi-billion dollar entertainment corporations are his personal friends.
- **Signature Line:** *"BRO! The new corporate mascot Funko Pop has a metallic variant! I need 3!"*

#### 7. The Zoomer (The Screen-Native Ironist)
- **Visuals:** Permed curly "broccoli fade" hair, pastel oversized hoodie, wireless white earbuds, holding iPhone with TikTok open, vape pen tucked behind ear.
- **Lore & Mentality:** Communicates exclusively in ironic slang (*"no cap"*, *"on god"*, *"rizz"*, *"gyatt"*, *"cooked"*). Cannot sit through a 3-minute video without subway surfers gameplay below it.
- **Signature Line:** *"Bro's attention span is cooked fr fr no cap on god."*

#### 8. The 30-Year-Old Boomer (The Lawn & Grill Master)
- **Visuals:** Receding hairline, white wraparound dad sunglasses, cargo shorts with phone belt holster, holding a can of Ultra White Monster Energy.
- **Lore & Mentality:** Reminisces about 90s video games (*"Yep, Quake was a good game *sip*"*), obsesses over lawn fertilizer schedules, cleans the barbecue grill at 7 AM on Saturdays.
- **Signature Line:** *"They just don't make lawnmower engines like they used to. *cracks open Monster can*"*

#### 9. The NPC (The Programmed Consensus)
- **Visuals:** Completely flat monochromatic grey skin (`#9ca3af`), dead hollow circle eyes, straight horizontal line mouth, completely devoid of wrinkles or expression.
- **Lore & Mentality:** Repeats the latest mainstream media talking points verbatim. When asked a follow-up question, displays an internal 404 error screen.
- **Signature Line:** *"CURRENT OPINION LOADED. COMMENCING MORAL SUPERIORITY PROTOCOL."*

#### 10. The Coomer (The Dopamine Depleted)
- **Visuals:** Red bloodshot sunken eyes, unshaven patchy scruff, greasy forehead, hunched posture, illuminated exclusively by blue screen light.
- **Lore & Mentality:** Dopamine receptors completely fried by infinite scrolling and digital overstimulation. Has forgotten what sunlight feels like.
- **Signature Line:** *"Just one more video at 3:45 AM... my eyes only hurt when I blink."*

## 5. Audio Architecture & Sonic Identity

Wojak Explainer uses **adaptive genre scoring** tailored to the mood of the specific scene:

1. **The Comedic Quirky Baseline (70% of videos):**
   - **BPM:** 124–130 BPM.
   - **Instrumentation:** Pizzicato strings, bouncy marimba, walking upright bass, light brushed snare.
   - **Mood:** Lighthearted, witty, conversational observation.

2. **The Doomer / Russian Post-Punk Soundscape (Nihilistic / Overworked scenes):**
   - **BPM:** 110–120 BPM.
   - **Instrumentation:** Chorus-heavy electric guitar, pulsing analog synth bassline (Juno/Minimoog), driving 4-on-the-floor 80s drum machine.
   - **Mood:** Cold winter night, cigarette smoke, existential fatigue.

3. **The GigaChad / Phonk Anthem (Alpha / Chad / Gym scenes):**
   - **BPM:** 135–145 BPM.
   - **Instrumentation:** Distorted 808 sub-bass, cowbell melody leads, aggressive sidechain compression.
   - **Mood:** Peak dominance, unbothered confidence.

4. **The Bloomer / Lofi Study Beat (Wholesome scenes):**
   - **BPM:** 80–85 BPM.
   - **Instrumentation:** Dusty electric piano (Rhodes), vinyl crackle, warm sub-bass, gentle jazz drums.
   - **Mood:** Morning coffee, fresh air, peaceful mindset.

### Audio Mastering Standard
- **EBU R128 Loudness:** Target `-14.0 LUFS` integrated loudness.
- **True Peak:** `-1.0 dBFS` to prevent clipping on YouTube transcoder.
- **SFX Mixing:** Surgical stingers (vine boom, monster can crack, anime ping, flatline) mixed at `-6 dBFS` relative to BGM ducking.

## 6. Implementation Blueprint in AutoShorts

To enable AutoShorts to generate Wojak Explainer style archetype videos alongside SapX country comparisons:

1. **New Script Subsystem (`wojak_archetypes.py`):**
   - Catalogs 10 popular archetype topics:
     - `types_of_introverts`
     - `types_of_gym_goers`
     - `every_generation`
     - `types_of_coders`
     - `types_of_students`
     - `types_of_drivers`
     - `types_of_music_listeners`
     - `types_of_dating_profiles`
     - `types_of_workers`
     - `types_of_gamers`
   - Each topic provides 6 to 10 curated archetype segments with exact punchlines, emotional tags, props, and setting backgrounds.

2. **Pipeline Integration:**
   - Both `autoshorts wojak --topic introverts` and `autoshorts longform --mode types_of` dynamically route through the archetype catalog.
   - Generates 16:9 landscape anthology videos or 9:16 vertical YouTube Shorts.
   - Renders with authentic Feels Guy / Doomer / Chad / Tradwife / Soyjak character bases.
